## TIER 1 — Idea (meetup discussion, admin-collected)
1. If you were CEO of Zimmer Biomet for a day, what's the first thing you'd change?
2. If you were site leader / departmental director for a week, what would you fix first?
3. What's one thing another department is doing that we should be doing?
4. What do you think other departments would say we do best – and worst?
5. What's the most valuable thing your department does that the rest of the business doesn't know about?
6. What's the one task in your week that feels like a waste of time – and how would you eliminate it?
7. Start / Stop / Continue: name one thing we should start doing, one we should stop, and one we should keep.
8. Where do things get "stuck" between your team and another team? What would unblock it?
9. If you had €10,000 -  €100,000 to improve your workplace, what would you spend it on?
10. What's one process you've seen at a previous employer that would work well here?
11. What's the most common mistake or block do you see – and what causes it?
12. What information do you need regularly that's hard to find or always arrives late?
13. How would you bring AI into your daily work if it were available tomorrow?
14. What's one repetitive task you'd hand over to a robot or AI without a second thought?
15. Which report, check or admin step could be automated so you can focus on more valuable work?
16. Have you used ZB AI Portal and what are your thoughts? How could it improve?
17. What data do we collect that we're probably not using well enough?
18. What's the one piece of technology or equipment that would make your job easier?
19. What's one thing that would make you look forward to coming to work more often?
20. Which two departments should talk to each other more, and about what?
21. What do new colleagues struggle with most in their first weeks – how could onboarding be better?
22. What skill would you love to learn that would also help the company?
23. How can management communicate better with the shop floor and commercial teams?
24. What's one small thing that would noticeably improve the experience for other departments?
25. If we could launch one new product, service or initiative in your department next year, what should it be?
26. A magic wand removes one rule, form or approval step tomorrow. Which one disappears, and what happens next?
27. Imagine a new colleague from the year 2040 visits Hazeldonk. What would make them laugh at how we work today?
28. If our biggest customer were a 12-year-old, how would you explain what we do – and what would they think is silly?
29. Pitch a completely ridiculous product or service for ZB. Then tell us the one serious idea hiding inside it.
30. If your department were a sports team, what position is unfilled and who should we sign?
31. You've been made Minister of Tuesdays. What's the one thing that would make every Tuesday at ZB better?
32. If we had to run the entire site with half the meetings, which ones survive and why?
33. A robot starts on your team on Monday. Write its job description in three lines.
34. What would you rename your job title to if it had to describe what you actually do?

## TIER 2 — Icebreaker (onboarding, shared only with meetup partners)
1. What did you want to be when you were 10 years old?
2. What was your very first job, and what did it teach you?
3. What's the best piece of career advice you've ever received?
4. What's something you're surprisingly good at that has nothing to do with your job?
5. What would your colleagues be surprised to learn about you?
6. What's your go-to way to switch off after a long day?
7. What's the best trip you've ever taken – and where's next on the list?
8. What's a hobby you've picked up (or dropped) in the last few years?
9. What's your favourite thing to cook or eat – and who makes it best?
10. Are you a morning person or a night owl – and does your job agree with that?
11. What's your most useless talent?
12. If you could have any animal as a colleague, which would it be and what job would it do?
13. What's the worst haircut or fashion choice you've ever made?
14. If you had to eat one meal every day for the rest of your life, what would it be?
15. What's a song you know every word to, even though you'd never admit it?
16. What's the strangest thing in your fridge or desk drawer right now?
17. Which fictional character would be the best (or worst) colleague?
18. If your life were a film, who would play you and what would it be called?
19. Which emoji do you overuse?
20. Would you rather work four 10-hour days or five 8-hour days?
21. Would you rather have unlimited coffee or unlimited holiday?
22. Would you rather be able to speak every language or play every instrument?
23. Beach, mountains or city?
24. Team teleport or team invisibility – and how would you use it at work?
25. What's a small thing that always makes your day better?
26. Which skill or hobby have you always wanted to try but never got around to?
27. What's the best concert, match or event you've ever been to?
28. If you could have dinner with anyone – alive or historical – who's at the table?
29. What's a tradition you love, and one you secretly don't get?
30. Cats, dogs, or "I have enough going on already"?
31. What's the last thing that made you laugh out loud?
32. If you won the lottery tomorrow, what's the first (sensible) thing and the first (not-so-sensible) thing you'd do?
33. What's your signature move at a party – dancing, DJ-ing, kitchen-hanging or early exit?
34. Which three words would your best friend use to describe you?
